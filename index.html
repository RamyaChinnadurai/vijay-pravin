From Tirunelveli to Munich
My story

From a police officer's son to “the NFT Police.”

I grew up in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu — my mother a teacher, my father in the police force. That mix of curiosity and a strong sense of justice has shaped everything I've built since.

After engineering at Anna University, I moved to Germany for a Master's at the Technical University of Munich, then spent years as a data scientist and analytics expert at Telefónica, Volkswagen and Siemens — turning messy data into decisions at global scale.

In the 2021 NFT boom I lost money to fraudulent collections — and found there were no tools to tell real from fake. So I built one. bitsCrunch uses AI to detect wash trading, counterfeits and manipulation, bringing forensic-grade transparency to digital assets.

“Be patient, persevere, and never stop learning — learning is the key.”
