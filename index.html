My story

A police officer's son who keeps building trust into technology.

I grew up in Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu. My mother was a teacher and my father served in the police force. That mix of curiosity and a strong sense of fairness has shaped everything I've built since.

After engineering at Anna University, I moved to Germany for a Master's at the Technical University of Munich, then spent years as a data scientist and analytics expert at Telefónica, Volkswagen and Siemens.

In the 2021 NFT boom I lost money to fraudulent collections and found no tools to tell real from fake. So I built one. bitsCrunch became "the NFT Police": AI forensics backed by Coinbase Ventures, Animoca Brands and Crypto.com Capital.

Today, as founder and CEO of HeyUla, I'm still doing what I love most: using data and AI to make everyday life simpler, and sharing what I learn along the way across 52 countries and counting.

"Be patient, persevere, and never stop learning. Learning is the key."
